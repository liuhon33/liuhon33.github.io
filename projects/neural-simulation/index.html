03Course project · University of Toronto

Exploring neural systems
through simulation.

In PSL 432, I used MATLAB to simulate neural control of arm movement and numeral recognition with deep-learning and reinforcement-learning models.

  • MATLAB
  • Deep learning
  • Reinforcement learning

Contribution Applying computational models to questions about learning and neural control.

Learning through models

The course project explored how computational models can represent aspects of movement control and recognition. Working in MATLAB connected my mathematics and human biology training through simulation.

Methods and context

The work used deep-learning and reinforcement-learning models for arm movement and numeral-recognition tasks. It was completed as coursework, rather than an independent clinical or production system.

PSL 432 · University of Toronto
January–April 2022 · Instructor: Prof. Douglas Tweed
